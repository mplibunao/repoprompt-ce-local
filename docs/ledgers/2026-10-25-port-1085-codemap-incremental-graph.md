For both launches, every window except the measured one was on `Default`, window 7 included: its switch to `Default` finished in 1 s on the idle app before the first launch. - Evidence, plan's rule: no sample from either build met the warm-reload rule. At steady state, every reload on both builds re-classified 6 of 29 catalog pages and reused stored graph-index entries for 1,754 of 1,810 candidates. The other 56 files never got a stored entry. A diagnostic load on the baseline showed every candidate processed (1,700 contributed, 109 empty, 1 excluded), and the manifest seal then held 1,556 records. So on this folder neither build can meet the rule. Each build also used the plan's three replacement samples (s4 to s6), and the rule rejected all of them for the same counts.
- Decision: after the baseline's samples and before any candidate sample, the comparison adds a steady-state rule. A load is steady when every root is ready, its builds, materializations, and locator and CAS fast paths don't change, and its classification and envelope-hit deltas equal the previous load's. Each build loads until it has three steady loads in a row, with at most 10 extra loads, and the 5% rule applies to the medians of those three when both builds' steady deltas match. Reason: the baseline's stored-entry hits rose over its first loads (0, then 1,552, 1,552, 1,554, 1,556, 1,658, 1,753, and 1,754), so two loads compare the same cache state only once that count stops changing. Source: orchestrator judgment, a departure from the plan.
- Evidence, result: both builds settled at the same per-load deltas, 6 classifications and 1,754 envelope hits for 1,810 candidates. The baseline's steady loads, s7 to s9, took 8.49, 7.27, and 8.50 s to ready (median 8.49 s) and 13.26, 12.04, and 14.12 CPU seconds (median 13.26). The candidate's, s1 to s3, took 5.05, 5.32, and 5.32 s (median 5.32 s) and 6.79, 7.03, and 6.85 CPU seconds (median 6.85), and its s4 to s6 took 4.80 to 5.36 s and 6.70 to 6.97 CPU seconds. The fastest baseline reload, 6.48 s and 10.88 CPU seconds, was slower than the slowest candidate reload. The candidate reused the baseline's stored entries from its first load, so item 5's split between manifest namespaces didn't recur.
- Evidence, cleanup: windows 1 through 7 went back to their original workspaces, and the disposable workspaces, the folders, and the three scratch worktrees were removed. The debug app keeps running the candidate merge build.
