# Run ledger: read freshness deadline

Request: #213 · Plan: `issue-213-read-freshness-deadline-2026-10-19.md`

Find out which wait a small `read_file` stalls in when it runs past the 30 s bounded tool deadline in a loaded window, make a backed-up ingress queue return the retryable `workspace_freshness_timeout` reply before the watchdog fires, and show that a window recovers from one timed-out read without an app restart. ## 1. Reproduce and confirm the stalled wait

- Decision: item 1 runs in two stages, the DEBUG settlement-snapshot op first and the reproduction recipe second, so the op passes review before the recipe's evidence depends on it. Source: orchestrator judgment.
- Decision: agents read source with shell tools on the worktree's paths and keep RepoPrompt structure-backed reads to a minimum. A session-bound worktree root carries a seeded-authority fence, so RepoPrompt reads under it enter the lock path that upstream PR 1003 fixes, and the production window this run worked in still had this bug. Source: orchestrator judgment.
- Evidence (production, build 55, 2026-10-19 05:10 UTC, from a #213 comment of that date): a production window with about 1,270 tabs reached the non-retryable released-provider limit about 20 minutes after an app restart. The busy reply to a `read_file` carried `last_handler_phase` `read_file.content_read`, `detached_age_ms` 268585, and `released_provider_count` 1. A 5 s process sample showed an idle main thread and no thread waiting on the Git authority or metadata-monitor locks that upstream PR 1003 reorders: the stuck provider was a suspended task inside `read_file.content_read`, not a thread blocked on those locks.
- Decision: the snapshot op reads the registry first and looks up the window only when the snapshot is empty, so a closed window's retained entries stay visible and a congested main actor doesn't delay the sample. Source: review finding.
- Decision: the op's success reply keeps `ok: true`, the envelope the neighbouring diagnostics ops share. Source: orchestrator judgment, declining a review nit.
- Decision: when the first snapshot is empty, the op takes the second snapshot right after the window lookup and returns `no_window` only when the window is gone and that snapshot is empty, so an entry admitted during the lookup isn't hidden. Source: follow-up review finding.
- Decision: no added `import RepoPromptDomainRuntime`, which the follow-up review asked for. The file names the RepoPrompt module's `MCPCodeStructureSettlementRegistry` typealias, and the module re-exports `RepoPromptDomainRuntime` (`Sources/RepoPrompt/Infrastructure/Diffing/RepoPromptDomainRuntimeExport.swift`). Source: orchestrator judgment, declining a review finding.
- Decision: the op keeps the shared `debugBoundedInt` parser, which the third review round found can trap on a floating-point `window_id` at or above 2^63. The defect predates this branch, affects every diagnostics op that uses the helper, exists only in DEBUG builds, and needs a deliberately out-of-range input. Source: orchestrator judgment, declining a review finding.
- Decision: the window check calls `WindowStatesManager.hasWindow(id:)` instead of repeating its predicate. The op keeps its own required-`window_id` parsing: the diagnostics extensions have no helper with that contract, and generalizing a file-private one would add API for one caller. Source: refactor analysis.
- Evidence (debug app, 2026-10-19 05:18–06:01 UTC, built from `main` at `a166df0b1` plus this item's op): a workspace with two plain-folder roots in a disposable scratch folder, `root-a` and `root-b`. A generator wrote a new directory of 200 one-line files into `root-a` every 0.1 s and deleted the oldest directory every 2 s. N = 200 produced the backlog without escalation.
  - 05:19 baseline: a `root-a` read took 0.133 s, the settlement snapshot read `0/0/0`, and both roots had no outstanding publications. The snapshot's three counts are active, detached, and released entries; active counts every entry the registry holds, including detached and released ones.
  - 05:21, after about 50 s of load: `root-a` had 153 outstanding publications, the oldest 36.5 s old.
  - 05:21:27: `read_file` of `root-a/seed/hello.txt` returned `tool_execution_timeout` after 30.45 s, settled as cancellation 0.2 s into the 5 s grace. The deadline trace named `read_file.content_read`, with a phase age of 30,100 ms at the deadline.
  - Time before the freshness wait: 0.489 ms from `ReadFile.ProviderEntered` to `ReadFile.ExplicitFreshnessBegan`, from the read-search capture's lifecycle events.
  - Right after the timeout: no busy reply, so no `last_handler_phase` from the registry. Four `root-b` probe reads returned at once (the first in 0.66 s). The settlement snapshot read `0/0/0` at every sample. `root-a` held 325–350 outstanding publications, one waiter, and an oldest publication 114–131 s old.
  - 05:24 and 05:45: two more `root-a` reads under backlog timed out at 30.92 s and 30.54 s, both settled during the grace.
  - Drain (generator stopped at 05:25 with about 500 publications outstanding): the apply rate fell from about 0.2 to 0.06 publications per second as `root-a` grew to about 2,750 generated directories. `rebuildRootCatalogShardAuthoritatively` dominated the process samples taken during the drain (807 of 1,048 samples in the shard's materialized-base build, 161 in its sort). Runtime-snapshot calls, which run on the store actor, took 10–17 s each, and a `root-b` read took 13.4 s, while the main thread was idle. The run stopped waiting for the drain at 05:57, with 263 publications outstanding.
  - 05:57:29, under the remaining backlog: a `root-a` read escaped. It detached at 35.5 s, still in `read_file.content_read`, and settled on its own 7.8 s after detach. Its trace put 2.4 s of work before `content_read` began. The 05:45 read's trace put 7.35 s there.
  - 05:58: a repeat with 1–3 s sampling showed `1/0/0` (its own reserved entry) only while in flight, settled during the grace at 33.03 s, then `0/0/0`.
  - Totals: of five `root-a` reads that timed out under backlog, one escaped for 7.8 s and the rest settled during the grace. No sample showed a detached or released entry, and no read of another root got a busy reply.
- Evidence (code, 2026-10-19): the freshness race `awaitAppliedIngressWithTimeout` (`Sources/RepoPrompt/Infrastructure/WorkspaceContext/WorkspaceFileContextStore.swift`) is an actor-isolated method of the store. Its timeout task and its cancellation handler resolve the race off the actor, but the awaiting method must run on the store actor again before it returns. While a long apply job holds the actor, both the timeout reply and the watchdog's cancellation wait for that job to finish.
- Decision: the plan's gate for items 2 and 3 expected a freshness-wait stall whose provider escapes, fences the window (`1/1/0`), and, once the 30 s horizon has passed, is marked released when the next call is admitted (`1/1/1` after that call settles). The first half held: the phase is `read_file.content_read`, under 10 s passes before the wait starts, the backlog is over 30 s, and one waiter is present. The escape pattern didn't occur, because the wait's cancellation settles within the grace unless an apply job holds the store actor longer than the grace. This run didn't reproduce the production wedge, an escape still running after 268 s: the longest escape here lasted 7.8 s. The gate required a re-plan of items 2 and 3, recorded under "Parked decisions." Source: approved plan.
- Evidence limit: the planned observation of the window for 120 s after the backlog drained didn't happen, because the drain projected to over an hour.

## 2. Freshness before the watchdog

- Decision: besides the 20 s bound and the typed retryable reply, the timed freshness wait's timeout and cancellation path no longer needs the store actor, so the reply and the watchdog's cancellation reach the caller while an apply job holds the actor. Source: the parked decision's answer.
- Decision: every timed freshness entry on the store is `nonisolated`, including the `file_actions` and `apply_edits` preflight entries that share the race, and root resolution runs inside the operation closure on the actor. The race resolves and resumes off the actor, so a timeout or a cancellation returns while an apply job holds it. Source: the parked decision's answer.
- Decision: the DEBUG timeout override lives in lock-protected storage on the store, so the race reads it without the actor, and an actor method sets it. Source: orchestrator judgment. The plan's actor-isolated property would have needed the actor.
- Decision: the held-ingress test runs every row over the real socket path, and drops the plan's direct `resolveReadFileRequestAfterFreshness` row, which the socket `read_file` row covers. Source: implementer judgment, accepted.
- Decision: a third test, `MCPReadMutationPathContractTests/testTimedFreshnessWaitsSettleWhileTheStoreActorIsHeld`, guards the off-actor contract: with the store actor blocked through an existing DEBUG hook, each timed entry still times out and a cancelled wait still throws `CancellationError`. It failed on `32ee7348a`, the commit before the fix. Source: orchestrator request, checked against `rpce-test-quality`.
- Decision: the actor-hold test owns and settles its tasks, and its cancellation row cancels a wait that is already registered. Source: review findings.
- Decision: no test catches a call site that passes the 30 s constant instead of the 20 s bound. The held-ingress table overrides the bound for speed, and the only stronger oracle is a real-time test of 20 s or more. Source: orchestrator judgment, declining a review observation.
- Decision: the activation and response-text helpers the held-ingress test shares with `WindowRoutedFileToolIntegrationTests` move to `MCPWindowTestSupport`, so neither suite depends on the other's test class. `MCPExportWatchdogIntegrationTests` drops its own `responseObject` for the shared one and keeps its other helpers. Source: refactor analysis, narrowed to the dependency this change introduced.
- Decision: the single-path explicit-request overload keeps its own root selection rather than calling `explicitRequestIngressRootIDs`. The duplication predates this change, and removing it would widen the distribution's divergence from upstream in a file that ports touch. Source: orchestrator judgment, declining a refactor finding.
- Deferred: the per-publication catalog-shard rebuild that saturates the store actor (`rebuildRootCatalogShardAuthoritatively`) is tracked in #222, related to upstream issue 1416. The same saturation still delays store calls that run before the freshness wait starts (`readFileBody`'s `rootRefs(scope:)`, `FrozenFileToolAuthority` capture and validation), which a cancellation can't interrupt, so `MCPTimeoutPolicy` describes the 10 s headroom as reserved for that work, not bounded.

## 3. Live validation and recovery evidence

- Evidence (debug app, 2026-10-19 07:56–08:15 UTC, built from `8cd9adbcf`): the same two-root workspace and generator as item 1, at N = 200.
  - Backlog phase. The read was issued at 07:57:56, with 144 publications outstanding and the oldest 38.1 s old. `read_file` of `root-a/seed/hello.txt` returned in 20.64 s with exit code 0, `error_code` `workspace_freshness_timeout`, and `retryable` true. The read reached the freshness wait 0.7 ms after entering the provider and spent 20.15 s in it. `get_file_tree` with `{"type":"roots"}` returned the typed `workspace_freshness_timeout:` error in 20.22 s. Reads of `root-b` took 0.13–1.52 s. The settlement snapshot read `1/0/0` only while a call was in flight and `0/0/0` otherwise.
  - Saturation phase. The generator ran 5 minutes (2,543 directories) and stopped at 08:02:05. From 08:08:35, runtime-snapshot calls took 5.4–6.4 s, against 10–17 s in item 1. Three `root-a` reads, at 08:09:08, 08:09:41, and 08:10:11, returned the typed retryable reply in 29.72 s, 21.37 s, and 22.65 s. Each spent about 20 s in the freshness wait, and 67 samples showed no detached entry.
  - Before the fix (item 1), every `root-a` read under backlog ended in `tool_execution_timeout` at 30.5–35.5 s, and one escaped. After it, no read reached the watchdog: stderr has no deadline-expired trace for the whole run.
  - The 29.72 s read spent 9.58 s before entering the `read_file` provider, inside `FrozenFileToolAuthority.validate`'s call to the store's `rootRefs(scope:)`. That is the pre-wait store work deferred to #222. Under heavier saturation it can still reach the watchdog.
  - `make dev-smoke` at 08:13:03 exited 0 in 1.9 s.
- Decision: `docs/testing.md` lists the `mcp_structure_settlement_snapshot` call beside the other DEBUG-only diagnostics. Source: approved plan.
- Evidence (recovery without a restart, from item 1's run on `main` before the fix): one timed-out read did not fence the window. After the 05:21:27 timeout settled during the grace, four `root-b` reads returned at once and the settlement snapshot stayed `0/0/0`. The one read that escaped, at 05:57:29, settled on its own 7.8 s after detach, and later reads in the window kept working. The app wasn't restarted. Neither the registry's 30 s horizon release nor its limit of one released provider came into play, and this branch doesn't change either.
